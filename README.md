Download portal 2 motion pack from here:  
https://www.moddb.com/games/portal-2/downloads/portal-2-sixense-motionpack-post-february-2021-setup
  
Controls:  

- Q for toggling telekinesis.  
- Click and hold LMB or RMB (depending on which portal you want to surf) looking at a portal with telekinesis mode enabled for portal surfing. 
- Mouse wheel for moving picked up cubes with telekinesis closer/further and for rotating a portal when portal surfing. 
- Enter for start button. (skip tutorial videos)
